Security

BLOCK is signed with post-quantum ML-DSA-44. BTC, LTC, DOGE and all EVM assets use ECDSA (secp256k1) and are not post-quantum. Every private key — post-quantum and ECDSA alike — is stored in a vault encrypted with a memory-hard KDF (scrypt) and authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM), and never leaves this device.
