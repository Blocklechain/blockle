"Planned" means exactly that: no deposits, no market, nothing to send. Each coin's page says where it stands. We would rather publish the queue than imply a market that is not open.

Why a venue for these

A coin can have a healthy network, a dozen pools and a few thousand miners, and still have nowhere to sell — because listing fees are written for projects with a treasury, and these chains mostly do not have one. So the mining carries on and the coin accumulates in wallets.

Listing here costs a conversation, not a fee that has to be funded. In exchange we are strict about the part that actually goes wrong: we run the node ourselves, we reconcile what customers are owed against what we hold on every pass, and we halt withdrawals on a shortfall rather than paying the fastest few people out of everyone else's coins.
