The question that decides most of the work is what your node is a fork of. A Bitcoin Core or go-ethereum fork reuses an adapter we already run. A bespoke codebase needs a custom one, and we will say so rather than quote a date we cannot keep.

We run our own node for every listed chain. We will not custody an asset against someone else's hosted RPC, because then their outage is our customers' frozen balance.
