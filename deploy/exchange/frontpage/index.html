What this exchange does differently

Three things worth knowing before you deposit anywhere, here included.

Fees are 0.20%, both sides

0.20% maker and 0.20% taker, taken from what you receive rather than charged separately. Fees round down, so the sub-unit stays with you.

Withdrawals are automatic

No manual review queue and no operator approving payouts. Limits are enforced in code — a per-request ceiling and a rolling 24-hour cap per account — so withdrawals are fast without being unbounded.

Books reconciled against custody

An append-only double-entry ledger, checked on a timer against what the custodian actually holds. If the two ever disagree, withdrawals of that asset halt automatically.

We would rather you self-custody

An exchange account is for trading. For holding, use the non-custodial wallet — we cannot freeze it, cannot move it, and cannot lose it for you.
