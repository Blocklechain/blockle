Sweep revenue needs a code

Moves accrued fees from the fee account into the house account, where they can be traded or withdrawn like any other balance. The gas float is held back automatically — sweeping it would book a profit the venue then cannot pay withdrawals out of.

Fund a gas float needs a code

Records a treasury transfer into the gas float. This moves the BOOKS, not the coins — send the asset to the custody wallet as well, or reconciliation will correctly report a shortfall.
