Three layers of protection

Advanced security without the jargon. You pick what to protect; Blockle Protect does the cryptography.

Layer 1 · On your device

Post-quantum encryption & signatures

Each file is encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM, its key wrapped with ML-KEM-768, and signed with ML-DSA-65 — the new U.S. standards built to resist quantum computers. We never see your files or keys.

Layer 2 · Your cloud

Works with Apple iCloud

Select existing iCloud files, documents or photos and protect them without changing how you work. We store an encrypted copy alongside — your original stays exactly where it is.

Layer 3 · Independent proof

Anchored on the Blockle chain

A tamper-evident fingerprint of each file is recorded on the Blockle blockchain — never the file itself. Anyone can verify a file is authentic and unchanged, without trusting us.

Honest about security

Strong, standards-based cryptography — described plainly, without overclaiming.

Standards, not invention

ML-KEM-768 (FIPS 203), ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204), AES-256-GCM, SHA-384 — audited implementations only. We never roll our own cryptography.

Post-quantum, not magic

Built to resist known quantum attacks under today's analysis. We say “post-quantum,” never “unhackable” or “quantum-proof.”

You hold the keys

Encryption and recovery keys live on your devices, protected by biometrics. Recovery is your own second key — there is no hidden backdoor.
