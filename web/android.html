How to install

Sideloading takes about a minute. Android will warn you because the app comes from outside the Play Store — that's expected for a direct download.

  1. Download the APK above on your Android phone (or transfer it from your computer).
  2. Open the file from your notification shade or the Files app. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser — tap Settings → Allow from this source.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway — you can verify the download with the SHA-256 checksum shown above.
  4. Open Blockle Wallet and create or import your wallet. Your keys are generated on the device and never leave it.
